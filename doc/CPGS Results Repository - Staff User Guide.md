## 1. About the Results Repository

The CPGS Results Repository is the official digital home of every postgraduate final result of the University, past and present. It replaces paper submission and paper storage of results, in line with memo **NAU/CPGS/004**, which called for three things:

1. **Scan and document all old (raw) results** before they deteriorate further.
2. **Set up a taskforce** to carry out the digitization.
3. **Receive all future results online** through one official channel.

The portal does all three, and adds secure search, official documents with QR verification, and a permanent record of every action taken.

### 1.1 Two ways results enter the system

| | **Old results (legacy digitization)** | **New results (online submission)** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **What** | Results already on paper in the archive | Results of current and future sessions |
| **Who enters them** | The Digitization Taskforce types them in from scanned paper sheets | Each department uploads its own results; nobody re-types them for the department |
| **Checked by** | A QA Reviewer, then the Head of Records | HOD, Faculty PG Sub-Dean, Head of Records, Provost |
| **Becomes final when** | The Head of Records locks the sheet | The Head of Records records the Senate approval date |

### 1.2 Principles you should know

- **Nothing becomes final on one person's word.** Every result is checked by at least one other person before it is locked. The person who typed a sheet can never pass it at QA, and the person who requests a correction can never approve it.
- **The signed paper sheet remains the legal source.** Every result in the system is linked to a scan of the signed, stamped sheet it came from.
- **Locked means final.** Once a sheet is locked, its results cannot be edited. A genuine error is corrected only through an **amendment** approved by the Provost, and the earlier version is kept.
- **Everything is recorded.** Every sign-in, view, download, approval and change is written to a tamper-evident audit log.
- **You only see what your role allows.** Departmental staff see only their own department; Faculty PG Sub-Deans see only their faculty.

## 2. Roles and what each can do

Each staff account has one role. The System Administrator assigns roles.

| Role | Typically held by | Main responsibilities in the portal | Sees |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **System Administrator** | CPGS ICT Unit | Creates and manages staff accounts and academic structure, manages stored files, reads the audit log. Can open every page and see every department, but **cannot** approve, QA, lock, index or submit results. | Everything (view) |
| **Provost, CPGS** | The Provost | Approves result sheets at the Provost stage, approves or rejects amendments, views reports and the audit log. | Everything |
| **Head, Records** | Deputy Registrar / Head of Records | Verifies sheets, records Senate approval dates, locks legacy sheets, issues statements and transcripts, requests amendments, manages files. | Everything |
| **Taskforce Lead** | Chair of the Digitization Taskforce | Creates archive boxes, assigns Indexers and QA Reviewers, monitors progress; can also index. | Everything |
| **Indexer** | Taskforce member | Uploads scans and enters (indexes) results for the boxes assigned to them. | Assigned boxes |
| **QA Reviewer** | Taskforce member | Checks indexed sheets against their scans and passes or returns them. | Assigned boxes |
| **Department PG Coordinator** | Departmental PG Coordinator | Prepares and submits the department's final results online. | Own department |
| **Head of Department** | HOD | Approves or returns the department's submissions. | Own department |
| **Faculty PG Sub-Dean** | Faculty PG Sub-Dean | Approves or returns submissions from departments in the faculty. | Own faculty |
| **Internal Auditor** | Internal Audit | Reads records and the audit log; cannot change anything. | Everything (read only) |

> **Employers and other institutions do not need an account.** They confirm documents on the public verification page (see [section 9.4](#94-public-verification)).

## 3. Getting started

### 3.1 Your account

The System Administrator creates your account using your **official university email address**. You receive an email with a **temporary password**; if you do not, ask the administrator, who can also show it to you once on screen.

### 3.2 Signing in for the first time

1. Open the portal and click **Staff sign in**.
2. Enter your email address and the temporary password, then click **Sign in**. Use the eye icon in the password box to show or hide what you have typed.
3. You are taken to **Set your password**. Enter a new password twice and click **Save password**. Your password must:
   - be at least **12 characters** long;
   - contain an **upper-case** letter, a **lower-case** letter and a **number**;
   - be different from the temporary password.
4. If two-step verification is required, you are then taken to **Set up two-step verification** (see 3.3).
5. You arrive at your **Dashboard**.

### 3.3 Setting up two-step verification

Staff accounts are protected by a second step: a 6-digit code from an authenticator app on your phone, in addition to your password.

1. Install an authenticator app on your phone, such as **Google Authenticator** or **Microsoft Authenticator**.
2. On the set-up page, click **Generate QR code**.
3. In the app, add an account and **scan the QR code**. If you cannot scan it, type in the key shown under the code.
4. Enter the 6-digit code the app shows and click **Verify and finish**.

From then on, after entering your password you will be asked for the current code from the app. Codes change every 30 seconds; always use the newest one.

> **Lost or changed your phone?** Ask the System Administrator to **reset your MFA**. You will set up the authenticator again at your next sign-in.

### 3.4 Everyday sign-in

1. Enter your email and password and click **Sign in**.
2. If asked, enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app and click **Verify and sign in**.

Your session lasts up to **8 hours**. Sign out when you leave your computer: click **Sign out** at the bottom of the left-hand menu.

### 3.5 If you cannot sign in

- **"Email, password or code is incorrect"**: check for typing errors and use the newest authenticator code.
- **"Too many attempts" or "Account locked"**: after 5 failed attempts the account is locked for **15 minutes**. Wait and try again.
- **Forgotten password**: the System Administrator can issue a new temporary password.

### 3.6 Changing your password later

Go to **My account → Change password**, enter your current password and the new one twice, then click **Save password**.

## 5. Old results: legacy digitization

**Who:** Taskforce Lead, Indexers, QA Reviewers, Head of Records.

### 5.1 Overview of the process

```
Rescue and box  →  Create box in portal  →  Assign Indexer and QA Reviewer
   →  Scan each sheet  →  Create sheet and upload scan  →  Enter results
   →  Send to QA  →  QA passes (or returns)  →  Head of Records locks  →  Final
```

### 5.2 Step 1: Rescue and inventory the paper files (Taskforce Lead)

Before scanning, move result files out of damp or pest-affected rooms into sealed boxes. Deal first with the most damaged and the oldest sessions.

Then record each physical box in the portal:

1. Open **Digitization**.
2. In **Inventory a box**, fill in:
   - **Box code**, in the format `CRR-BOX-0001` (number boxes in sequence);
   - **Department** whose results are in the box;
   - **Sessions in the box** (hold **Ctrl**, or **⌘** on a Mac, to select several);
   - **Estimated sheets**;
   - **Priority**, from 1 (most urgent) to 5;
   - **Physical condition**: Good, Fair, Damaged, or Severely damaged;
   - **Storage location** and any **Notes**.
3. Click **Create box**.
4. On the box page, click **Print** under **Box label** and stick the QR label on the physical box.

### 5.3 Step 2: Assign the team (Taskforce Lead)

On the box page, under **Assignments**:

1. Choose the **Indexer**: a user with the Indexer or Taskforce Lead role.
2. Choose the **QA reviewer**: a user with the QA Reviewer or Head of Records role.
3. Click **Save assignments**.

The Indexer and QA Reviewer **must be different people**; the system will not accept the same person for both.

### 5.4 Step 3: Scan the sheets

Follow this standard for every sheet:

- **300 dpi** minimum, in **colour**, so stamps and signatures are clear;
- **one PDF per result sheet**, with all its pages, saved as **PDF/A** where possible;
- use a flatbed scanner for fragile pages;
- check that each page is the right way up and nothing is cut off.

### 5.5 Step 4: Create the sheet and upload the scan (Indexer)

1. Open **Digitization**, then click your box code.
2. Under **Start a result sheet from this box**, pick the **Programme** and **Session**, and click **Create sheet**.
3. On the sheet page, drag the scanned PDF into **Upload scanned pages** under **Source scan**, or click it to choose the file.
4. Wait for **✓ Saved**. The scan appears in the viewer.

When a file is uploaded, the system records a **checksum**: a digital fingerprint of the file that reveals if it is ever altered.

### 5.6 Step 5: Enter the results (Indexer)

With the scan on the left, enter the graduands on the right in either of two ways.

#### Option A: import a spreadsheet (recommended for many graduands)

1. In **Import from a spreadsheet (Excel or CSV)**, download a template: **Excel template (with courses)** or **CSV template**.
2. Type the graduands from the scan into the template, one row per graduand:

   | Column | Required | Notes |
   | --- | --- | --- |
   | Matric No | Yes | Exactly as on the sheet. Leading zeros are kept. |
   | Surname | Yes | |
   | Other Names | Yes | |
   | Sex (M/F) | No | M or F |
   | CGPA | No | 0.00 to 5.00 |
   | Class of Degree | Yes | Distinction, Credit, Merit, Pass, Fail or Not applicable |
   | Thesis Title | No | |
   | Supervisor | No | |
   | Source Page | No | Page of the scan the row was read from |
   | Illegible (Y/N) | No | Y if part of the row cannot be read |
   | Remarks | No | |

   In the Excel template you may also list each graduand's courses on the **Courses** sheet: matric number, course code, title, units, score and grade. Do not rename sheets or column headings.
3. Under **Rows were read from scan**, select the scan the rows came from.
4. Choose the filled file and click **Validate and import**.

**What happens next:**

- Every row is checked. **If any row has a problem, nothing is saved**, and a table lists each problem by row and column. Correct the file and import it again.
- Rows whose matric number is already on the sheet are **updated**, new matric numbers are **added**, and existing rows are **never deleted**. You can import in batches, or fix mistakes by re-importing.

#### Option B: enter graduands one by one

Use **Or index graduands one by one**:

1. Enter the **Matric no**, **Surname** and **Other names** (required), and **Sex**, **CGPA**, **Class of degree**, **Thesis title** and **Supervisor**.
2. Under **Read from scan**, choose the scan, and enter the **Page**.
3. Optionally open **Courses** and enter one course per line, in the form:
   `AEX801 | Research Methods | 3 | 72 | A`
   (code | title | units | score | grade)
4. Add any **Remarks**.
5. Click **Save row**.

To correct a row, enter it again with the same matric number and save; it is updated. To remove a wrongly entered row, click **Delete** beside it in the graduands table.

#### Checks and warnings

| The system tells you if… | Type | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The matric number already has a record for this programme on another sheet | Error (not saved) | Check whether the result was already indexed |
| The matric number belongs to a student of another department | Error (not saved) | Check the matric number and the box |
| The matric number does not match the department's format | Error (not saved) | Check for typing errors |
| The CGPA does not normally give that class of degree | Warning (saved) | Re-check the scan; enter exactly what the sheet says |
| A course grade does not match its score | Warning (saved) | Re-check the scan |

#### Illegible entries

**Never guess.** If part of a row cannot be read, tick **Part of this row is illegible** (or put `Y` in the Illegible column). The row is flagged for the Head of Records, who must deal with it before the sheet can be locked.

### 5.7 Step 6: Send to QA (Indexer)

When every graduand on the sheet has been entered and checked against the scan:

1. Make sure the scan is attached.
2. Click **Indexing complete: send to QA** and confirm.

The sheet becomes **Awaiting QA** and can no longer be edited unless it is returned.

### 5.8 Step 7: QA review (QA Reviewer)

1. Open **QA review**. Sheets from boxes assigned to you are listed. You never see sheets you indexed yourself.
2. Open a sheet and compare **every row** against the scan: matric number, name, CGPA, class of degree and thesis title.
3. Then either:
   - **pass it**: tick **I compared all … row(s) against the scan** and click **Pass QA**; or
   - **return it**: write what must be corrected in the comment box and click **Return to indexer**.

A returned sheet goes back to the Indexer with your comment shown at the top. After correction it comes back to you.

### 5.9 Step 8: Verify and lock (Head of Records)

1. QA-passed sheets appear on the **Digitization** page under **Ready to verify and lock**, and on your Dashboard.
2. Open a sheet and spot-check rows against the scan.
3. If any rows are flagged **Illegible**, resolve them first. To lock anyway, tick **Lock despite … illegible row(s)**.
4. Click **Lock sheet**.

The records are now **final and searchable**, and can only be changed through an amendment.

If you find problems, open **Return to taskforce instead**, describe what must be corrected and click **Return**.

### 5.10 Certified transcription for signing (legacy sheets)

The taskforce can print a **certified transcription** of a legacy sheet: a formal record that the typed results match the original paper sheet in the archive box.

**Where to find it:**

- **While indexing**: at the bottom of the **Import from a spreadsheet** card, click **Download certified transcription (PDF)**.
- **At any time**: next to **Row fingerprint** under **Details and history** on the sheet page, for anyone who can view the sheet.

**What it contains:**

- the heading "Certified transcription of legacy result sheet", with the faculty, department, programme, session and archive box code;
- a numbered table of graduands: matric number, name, sex, CGPA, class of degree and thesis title;
- a certification that the rows were transcribed from the original sheet in that box and checked against it, and that the original and its scan remain the legal source;
- signature boxes for the **Indexer**, **QA Reviewer** and **Taskforce Lead**, and a **Records Unit stamp** box;
- a footer with the sheet reference, the **row fingerprint** and the page number.

The QA and lock panels show the current row fingerprint, so reviewers can confirm that a signed copy matches the rows in the system. See [section 7.4](#74-the-row-fingerprint).

### 5.11 Managing boxes

- The **Digitization** page lists all boxes with their department, sessions, team and progress, plus counts of sheets being indexed, awaiting QA and locked, and the **QA return rate**.
- Indexers and QA Reviewers see only the boxes assigned to them.
- On a box page, the Taskforce Lead can change the box **status** (Inventoried, In progress, Completed). A box can only be marked **Completed** when every sheet in it is locked.

## 6. New results: online submission and approval

**Who:** Department PG Coordinator, Head of Department, Faculty PG Sub-Dean, Head of Records, Provost.

> From go-live, departments submit final results **only** through the portal. Paper submissions are no longer accepted.

### 6.1 The approval chain

```
PG Coordinator submits
   →  Head of Department
   →  Faculty PG Sub-Dean
   →  Head of Records (records verification)
   →  Provost
   →  Head of Records records the Senate approval date  →  all records locked
```

- At each stage, the officer receives an **email** and the sheet appears in their **Approvals** inbox.
- **Anyone in the chain can return the sheet** with a reason. It goes back to the department with its full history kept.
- If a sheet waits more than **7 days** at any stage, the officer receives a **reminder email**, and the sheet is highlighted on the Dashboard.

### 6.2 Preparing and submitting results (PG Coordinator)

#### Before you start: create the submission

1. Open **Submissions** and click **New submission**.
2. Choose the **Programme** and **Session** and click **Create draft and continue**.

Only one open submission is allowed per programme and session.

You are taken to the sheet page, where the **Prepare and submit** panel takes you through four numbered steps.

#### Step 1: Download and fill the template

Click **Download template (.xlsx)**, or **Download CSV template** if you prefer CSV.

- **Graduands sheet**: one row per graduand, with Matric No, Surname, Other Names, Sex (M/F), CGPA, Class of Degree, Thesis Title and Supervisor.
- **Courses sheet** (Excel only): one row per course taken, with Matric No, Course Code, Course Title, Units, Score and Grade. Repeat the graduand's matric number on each of their course rows.

Rules:

- Do not rename the sheets or change the column headings.
- Class of Degree must be one of: Distinction, Credit, Merit, Pass, Fail or Not applicable.
- CGPA is on a 5-point scale with two decimal places.
- The **Instructions** sheet in the template shows a completed example.

#### Step 2: Upload the filled template

Choose the file and click **Validate and save**.

- Every cell is checked first. **If anything is wrong, nothing is saved**, and a table shows each problem by sheet, row and column.
- Uploading again **replaces all the rows** on the sheet.
- Warnings, for example a CGPA that does not normally give the stated class, are shown for you to double-check but do not stop the upload.

#### Step 3: Print, sign, stamp and attach the result sheet

1. Click **Download sheet for signing (PDF)**. This is built from the rows you uploaded; see [section 7.3](#73-the-sheet-for-signing-online-submissions).
2. Print it and have it signed by the **PG Coordinator**, **Head of Department** and **External Examiner**, then **stamped** by the department.
3. Scan it (see [section 7.2](#72-what-makes-a-good-scan)).
4. Drag the scan into **Attach the signed and stamped result sheet**, beside the scan viewer.

#### Step 4: Submit for approval

1. Tick **I confirm the … row(s) below match the signed and stamped result sheet**.
2. Click **Submit to HOD**.

The status changes to **Awaiting HOD** and the HOD is notified.

#### If your sheet is returned

- You receive an email, and the sheet shows a red notice with **who returned it, when and why**.
- **Submissions** shows it under **Needs your action**, and a badge appears in the menu.
- Correct the template and upload it again. If the rows changed, **print and sign a new sheet for signing**, scan it and attach it, then click **Resubmit to HOD**.
- The sheet keeps the same reference number and its full history. Approvers are shown exactly which rows changed since the last submission.

### 6.3 Reviewing and approving (HOD, Faculty PG Sub-Dean, Head of Records, Provost)

1. Open **Approvals**. **Waiting for you** lists sheets at your stage. Sheets waiting over 7 days are highlighted.
2. Open a sheet. You will see:
   - the **source scan** on the left;
   - **Your decision**, showing what changed since any previous submission and the **row fingerprint** to check;
   - the **graduands** table, with warnings such as **Check class**;
   - **Details and history**: every previous submission, approval and return, with names, dates and comments.
3. Check the rows against the signed scan. Confirm that the scan is **signed and stamped**, and that its footer shows the **same row fingerprint** as the page.
4. Then either:
   - click **Approve**, adding an optional comment; or
   - write the reason in **Comment** and click **Return to department**. A reason is required.

**Records verification (Head of Records):** at the **Awaiting Records** stage, compare every row with the attached signed scan before approving.

**Senate approval (Head of Records):** when a sheet reaches **Awaiting Senate date**:

1. Enter the **Senate approval date**. It cannot be in the future.
2. Click **Record Senate approval and lock**.

Every record on the sheet becomes **final and locked**, and the department is notified.

### 6.4 What you will see after acting

The decision panel disappears once you have acted, because the sheet has moved to the next stage. A green **"✓ Done"** notice at the top confirms what you did and the sheet's new status. **Recently acted on by you** in Approvals lists your recent decisions.

## 7. The source scan and the sheet for signing

### 7.1 What the source scan is

The **source scan** is a scanned copy of the **signed and stamped** paper result sheet. The spreadsheet rows are the typed data; the scan is the official signed document behind them. Approvers compare the two line by line, and the scan is kept permanently as the **legal source**.

For an online submission, the scan should show:

- the heading: Nnamdi Azikiwe University, the faculty, the department, the programme and the session;
- one row per graduand, with matric number, name, CGPA, class of degree and thesis title;
- the signatures and dates of the PG Coordinator, the Head of Department and, where applicable, the External Examiner;
- the official **department stamp**.

**Why both are required:** the typed rows make results searchable and let the system check them automatically; the signed scan is the evidence that the results were properly approved. A submission cannot be sent without both.

### 7.2 What makes a good scan

| | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| **Format** | PDF, with all pages of the sheet in one file. PDF/A preferred; JPG or PNG accepted. |
| **Size** | Up to 50 MB per file |
| **Quality** | At least 300 dpi, in colour, so the stamp and signatures are clearly visible |
| **Completeness** | Every page, the right way up, nothing cut off |
| **Equipment** | A scanner or a proper scanning app. An ordinary phone photo is usually not good enough. |
| **Matching** | Every graduand in the upload must appear on the scan with the same values |

### 7.3 The sheet for signing (online submissions)

The **Download sheet for signing (PDF)** button produces a print-ready result sheet from the rows you uploaded, so every department's sheet looks the same and always matches the data.

It appears in step 3 of every draft or returned submission once the template has been uploaded. Until then the page says **"Upload the filled template first"**.

**What the PDF contains (A4 landscape):**

- the University and CPGS heading, sheet reference, faculty, department, programme and session;
- a numbered table of graduands: matric number, name (surname first), sex, CGPA, class of degree and the full thesis title, with column headings repeated on every page;
- on the last page, the total number of graduands, a certification statement, signature boxes (name, signature, date) for the **PG Coordinator**, **Head of Department** and **External Examiner**, and a box for the **department stamp**;
- a footer on every page with the reference, the **row fingerprint**, the date generated and "Page x of y".

### 7.4 The row fingerprint

The **row fingerprint** is a short code, such as `7B4C-F2DF`, calculated from the results on a sheet. It is printed on every sheet for signing and certified transcription, and shown on the sheet page.

- **For departments and indexers:** if the rows change after printing, the fingerprint changes. Print and sign a fresh copy.
- **For approvers and reviewers:** check that the footer of the attached signed scan shows the **same code** as the sheet page. A different code means the data changed after the sheet was signed. **Return the sheet.**

## 8. Searching and viewing records

### 8.1 Searching

Open **Search records**.

- Type a **matric number**, a **surname or other name**, or words from a **thesis title**, then click **Search**.
- Narrow the results with the filters: **Faculty**, **Department**, **Programme**, **Session**, **Class of degree**, **Legacy or online**, and **Locked (final) or In progress**.
- Click **Clear filters** to start again.

Departmental staff see only their own department, and Faculty PG Sub-Deans only their faculty.

The Provost, Head of Records and System Administrator can click **Export CSV** to download the current search results. Exports are recorded in the audit log.

### 8.2 The record page

Click a matric number to open a graduand's record. It shows:

- **Final result**: CGPA, class of degree and Senate approval date;
- faculty, department, programme, session, supervisor and thesis title, with a link to the **result sheet** it came from;
- **Courses**, if recorded;
- the **source scan**, opened at the scan the record was read from;
- the sheet's **approval history**;
- a **Locked · v1** label (or v2, v3… after amendments), or **In progress** if not yet final;
- for authorised staff, **Issued documents**, **Request an amendment**, and earlier **versions** of the record.

Every record view is recorded in the audit log.

### 8.3 Viewing scans

Scans open inside the portal through a **secure link that expires after 5 minutes**. If a scan stops showing after a while, reload the page. Every view is recorded.

## 9. Statements of result, transcripts and verification

**Who issues:** Head of Records (and the System Administrator).

### 9.1 Issuing a document

Documents can only be issued for **locked (final)** records that are not flagged illegible.

1. Open the graduand's record.
2. In **Issue a statement of result or transcript**, choose:
   - **Document**: Statement of result, or Academic transcript (which includes courses);
   - **Valid for**: 90 days, 1 year or 5 years;
   - **Issued to**: who the document is for, for example the graduand or a named employer;
   - **Email the document to** (optional): sends the recipient a link to download it, valid for 7 days.
3. Click **Generate document**.

The document is given a unique **verification code** (for example `ABCD-2345`) and a **QR code**.

### 9.2 What the document contains

- the University and CPGS letterhead;
- the graduand's name, matric number, degree, department, faculty and session;
- CGPA, class of degree, Senate approval date and thesis title;
- for transcripts, the list of courses with units, scores and grades;
- the QR code, verification code, issue date and expiry date.

### 9.3 Managing issued documents

- On the record page, **Issued documents** lists each document with its code, type, recipient, date and status (**Valid**, **Expired** or **Revoked**). Use **Download PDF** to obtain it again.
- To withdraw a document, for example if it was issued in error, click **Revoke**, give a reason and click **Confirm revoke**. The verification page shows it as revoked immediately.
- **Issued documents** in the menu lists all documents issued, how many have been revoked, and how many times documents have been checked on the verification page. Search by code with **Find code**.

### 9.4 Public verification

Anyone, such as an employer, another university or an embassy, can confirm a document without an account:

1. Go to the portal and click **Verify a result**, or scan the **QR code** on the document.
2. Enter the 8-character code and click **Verify**.

| Result | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Valid document** | Genuine and currently valid. Compare the name, degree, class and session with the document in hand. |
| **Expired** | Genuine, but its validity period has ended. Request a new one. |
| **Revoked** | Withdrawn by the Records Unit. Do not rely on it. |
| **Superseded** | The record was amended after issue. Request a new document. |
| **Not verified** | No document with this code was issued by CPGS. It may not be genuine. |

The verification page shows only the name, degree, class of degree, session, Senate approval date and document dates. It **never** shows scores, scans or contact details.

## 10. Correcting a locked record: amendments

Locked records cannot be edited directly. A genuine error, for example a CGPA mis-typed or an incorrect class of degree, is corrected through an **amendment**:

```
Head of Records requests (with evidence)  →  Provost approves or rejects
```

### 10.1 Requesting an amendment (Head of Records)

1. Open the graduand's record.
2. In **Request an amendment**, change only the values that are wrong: **CGPA**, **Class of degree**, **Thesis title**, **Supervisor**, **Senate approval date** or **Remarks**.
3. Write the **reason for the correction** (at least 10 characters).
4. Upload the **evidence**, for example a Senate extract or a corrected signed sheet.
5. Click **Send to Provost**.

Only one amendment can be pending for a record at a time. The Provost is notified by email.

### 10.2 Deciding an amendment (Provost)

1. Open **Amendments**. Each pending request shows the **current** and **proposed** values side by side, the reason and the evidence.
2. Click **Approve amendment**, or write a comment and click **Reject**. A comment is required to reject.

**Rules:**

- **You cannot approve your own request.** If you requested an amendment yourself, a yellow notice explains that another officer with the Provost role must decide it. This ensures no single person can change a final result.
- Staff who are not Provost see **"Awaiting a decision by the Provost."**

### 10.3 What happens on approval

- The record is updated as a **new version** (v2, v3…). **Earlier versions remain readable** on the record page.
- Any statements or transcripts already issued for that record are **revoked automatically**, because they no longer match. Issue new ones if needed.
- The change, the reason and the approver are recorded in the audit log.

## 11. Administration

### 11.1 The System Administrator's access

The System Administrator can open **every page** and see **every department's** data, including an **Approvals overview** of every sheet in the chain and every department's **Submissions** (view only). The administrator can also:

- create and manage **staff accounts** and the **academic structure**;
- manage **stored files**;
- create archive **boxes** and assign Indexers and QA Reviewers;
- issue and revoke **documents**;
- **export** records;
- read the **audit log**.

To protect the integrity of results, the administrator **cannot**:

- approve or return a result sheet;
- pass QA or lock a sheet;
- index a box or prepare a department's submission;
- request or approve an amendment.

These remain with the officer whose job it is. Every administrator action is recorded in the audit log.

### 11.2 Managing staff accounts (System Administrator)

Open **Users**.

**Finding accounts:**

- The summary chips at the top show **All accounts**, **Inactive**, **Active without MFA**, **Temporary password** and **Locked out**. Click one to list those accounts.
- Search by **name or email**, and filter by **role**, **faculty**, **department**, **status** (active or inactive) and **security** (MFA enabled, no MFA, temporary password, locked out, never signed in). Sort by role, name, last sign-in or newest account. Click **Clear** to reset.

**Creating an account:**

1. Under **Create account**, enter the **full name** and **official email**, and choose the **role**.
2. Choose the **department** for a **Head of Department** or **Department PG Coordinator**, or the **faculty** for a **Faculty PG Sub-Dean**. University-wide roles need neither.
3. Click **Create account**.

The temporary password is **shown once** on screen and emailed to the user.

**Managing an account:** click **Manage** beside it to:

- change the **role** and unit, then **Save role**;
- **Reset password**: issues a new temporary password, shown once (give it to the user in person);
- **Reset MFA**: removes the user's authenticator, for example after a lost phone;
- **Deactivate** or **Reactivate**: a deactivated user is signed out immediately and cannot sign in.

> Give each person their own account with one role. Do not share accounts or switch one account between roles: approvals and amendments depend on knowing exactly who acted.

### 11.3 Academic structure (System Administrator)

Open **Academic structure** to manage faculties, departments, programmes, sessions and the grading bands. Click any item to open its panel, with **Save changes** and **Delete**. Use the **Add…** forms to create new items.

| Item | You can edit | You can delete |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Faculty** | Name, code | Only when it has no departments or staff |
| **Department** | Name, code, matric number format, faculty | Only when nothing uses it |
| **Programme** | Name, code, degree type; department only while it has no results | Only when nothing uses it |
| **Session** | Year, only while unused | Only when nothing uses it |
| **Grading bands** | Minimum CGPA for Distinction, Credit, Merit and Pass | Not applicable |

**Rules that protect the records:**

- When an item is in use, **Delete** is replaced by an explanation, for example *"Cannot delete: in use by 4 students, 3 programmes, 4 graduand records"*. Deleting it would leave permanent results pointing at nothing.
- **Moving a department** to another faculty also moves its staff accounts, so Faculty Sub-Deans see the right departments.
- **Renaming a programme** does not change degree titles already recorded; existing records keep the title they were issued with.
- **Grading bands** must run Distinction > Credit > Merit > Pass > 0; an inverted table is rejected. Once saved, the bands are used immediately for the CGPA/class checks, and the notice changes from yellow "provisional" to green **"Official bands saved"** with the date.
- The **matric number format** of a department is an optional pattern that matric numbers must match; ask the ICT Unit to set it.
- Every create, edit and delete is recorded in the audit log with before and after values.

> **Grading bands are provisional until confirmed.** The default bands are Distinction ≥ 4.50, Credit ≥ 3.50, Merit ≥ 3.00, Pass ≥ 2.50. The Records Unit should confirm the official CPGS grading table, and the administrator should save it. Mismatches are only ever shown as warnings; the system never changes a class of degree by itself.

### 11.4 File storage (System Administrator, Head of Records; Provost and Auditor view only)

Open **File storage** to search, view and manage every scan and evidence file, without needing the storage provider's dashboard.

| | System Administrator | Head of Records | Provost, Auditor |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Search, open and download | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Verify integrity, run the storage check | ✓ | ✓ | |
| Delete unlinked files | ✓ | | |

**What the page shows:**

- **Summary cards**: total files and size, files not attached to anything, files awaiting backup, and storage usage.
- **Search and filters**: by file name, storage path or checksum; by purpose (legacy scan, submission scan, amendment evidence); by department; linked or unlinked; and storage type.
- **For each file**: name, type, size and pages; who uploaded it and when; its checksum and backup status; and what it is attached to, with links to its result sheet(s), the number of graduand rows read from it, or the amendment it supports.

**Actions:**

- **Open / Download**: through 5-minute secure links. Every opening is recorded.
- **Verify**: re-reads the stored file and confirms it matches the checksum recorded at upload, or warns that it has been altered or is missing. Report any mismatch to Internal Audit immediately.
- **Delete**: only for files attached to nothing, such as a scan uploaded by mistake and then removed from its sheet. A reason is required and the deletion is recorded. **Files attached to a sheet, record or amendment cannot be deleted**, because they are the legal source.
- **Run storage check**: compares the file store with the portal's register and lists any stored file with no matching record (usually an interrupted upload). The administrator can then remove it.
- **Lost files**: files saved before permanent storage was configured are marked **Lost: re-upload**. Ask the uploader to upload them again.

### 11.5 The audit log (System Administrator, Provost, Internal Auditor)

Open **Audit log** to see every recorded action: sign-ins, failed sign-ins, record and file views, uploads, approvals, returns, locks, amendments, document issues and revocations, exports, and administrative changes.

- Filter by **action**, **actor name**, **entity ID** and **date range**.
- Each entry shows the time, the person, the action, what changed (before and after), the IP address and a hash.
- Click **Verify chain integrity** to check that no entry has been altered or deleted. Each entry is linked to the one before it, so any tampering breaks the chain. The system also runs this check every night and alerts the administrator and Internal Audit if it fails.

### 11.6 My account (everyone)

**My account** shows your profile, role and unit, your last sign-in, links to **Change password** and two-step verification, and your **recent activity**. To change your name, role or unit, contact the System Administrator.

## 12. Security, privacy and the audit trail

### 12.1 Your responsibilities

- **Never share your password or authenticator codes.** Every action taken with your account is recorded as yours.
- **Sign out** when you leave your computer, especially on shared machines.
- **Do not download** scans or records to personal devices unless your duties require it. Downloads are recorded.
- **Report** anything suspicious, such as an unexpected sign-in on your account or a record you did not change showing as changed, to the ICT Unit and the Head of Records immediately.

### 12.2 How results are protected

- **Two-step verification** for staff, and account lockout after repeated failed sign-ins.
- **Role-based access**: departmental staff see only their department, faculty staff only their faculty.
- **Maker-checker**: indexers cannot QA their own work, and amendment requesters cannot approve them.
- **Locked records** can only change through an approved amendment, and earlier versions are kept.
- **Private file storage**: scans have no public link and open only through short-lived secure links. Each file's checksum detects any alteration.
- **Tamper-evident audit log**, checked every night.

### 12.3 Data protection

Personal data is processed under the **Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023**. The portal holds only what a result requires: no phone numbers or home addresses are stored on graduand records. Academic records are kept permanently, and audit logs for at least seven years. The public verification page never shows scores, scans or contact details. The full **Privacy notice** is linked at the bottom of the sign-in and verification pages.

## 13. Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **"Email, password or code is incorrect"** | Check your typing, use the eye icon to view the password, and use the newest authenticator code. |
| **"Too many attempts" / "Account locked"** | Wait 15 minutes and try again, or ask the administrator. |
| **Lost phone or new phone** | Ask the administrator to **Reset MFA**, then set up the authenticator again at sign-in. |
| **"You do not have access to that page"** | Your role does not include that page. Contact the administrator if you believe your role is wrong. |
| **Upload refused: "File storage is not configured on this server"** | Contact the ICT Unit. Uploads are disabled until storage is configured. |
| **Scan shows "This scan is no longer available… Please re-upload it"** | The file was saved before permanent storage was set up. The uploader should upload it again. If the sheet is no longer editable, the Head of Records can return it for the re-upload. |
| **Scan viewer says "Opening secure link…" for a long time, or "link has expired"** | Reload the page. Links last 5 minutes. |
| **"N problem(s) found. Nothing was saved."** after uploading a template | Read the problem table (sheet, row, column), correct the file and upload it again. Do not rename sheets or headings. |
| **"Matric number … belongs to a student in another department"** | Check the matric number and that you are working in the correct department, box or programme. |
| **"… already has a record for this programme"** | The result was already entered on another sheet. Check before entering it again. |
| **"There is already an open sheet for this programme and session"** | Continue with the existing sheet listed in **Submissions** instead of creating a new one. |
| **"Attach the signed and stamped result sheet scan first"** | Upload the signed scan in the **Source scan** box before submitting. |
| **"Confirm that the rows match the signed result sheet"** | Tick the confirmation box above **Submit to HOD**. |
| **"This sheet is not at your stage"** | The sheet is waiting for another officer. Check its status label. |
| **No Approve button on an amendment** | You requested it yourself; another Provost must decide it. |
| **Cannot delete a faculty, department, programme, session or file** | It is in use; the page explains by what. Items in use are protected. |
| **"Only locked (final) records can be issued"** | The sheet must be locked or Senate-approved before documents can be issued. |
| **Signed scan shows a different row fingerprint from the sheet page** | The rows changed after signing. Download and sign a fresh sheet, or (approvers) return the sheet. |
| **Emails not arriving** | Check Spam or Promotions folders. If nothing arrives, contact the ICT Unit. |

## 14. Frequently asked questions

**Do employers need an account to check a certificate or transcript?**
No. They use **Verify a result** on the portal home page, or scan the QR code on the document.

**Can a department edit results after submitting?**
Not while the sheet is in the approval chain. If a change is needed, ask the officer currently holding it to **return** it; the department can then correct and resubmit.

**Can results be changed after they are locked?**
Only through an **amendment**, requested by the Head of Records with evidence and approved by the Provost. The earlier version is kept.

**Why must I upload both a spreadsheet and a scan?**
The spreadsheet makes the results searchable and checkable; the signed, stamped scan is the legal proof that they were properly approved.

**What if part of an old result sheet cannot be read?**
Mark the row **Illegible**. Never guess. The Head of Records will resolve it before the sheet is locked.

**Why can't the person who typed a sheet also check it?**
So that every result is confirmed by at least two people. This is a basic safeguard for official records.

**Why can't the System Administrator approve results?**
The administrator manages the system, not the academic decisions. Approvals stay with the officers responsible for them.

**I received a reminder email. What does it mean?**
A sheet has been waiting at your stage for more than 7 days. Open **Approvals** to act on it.

**What happens to documents already issued when a record is amended?**
They are revoked automatically, and the verification page shows them as superseded or revoked. New documents should be issued from the updated record.

## 15. Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Amendment** | An approved correction to a locked record, creating a new version |
| **Approval chain** | The sequence of officers who review an online submission |
| **Audit log** | The permanent, tamper-evident record of every action in the portal |
| **Box** | A physical box of archived result files, recorded in the portal as a batch for digitization |
| **Certified transcription** | A signed PDF certifying that typed legacy results match the original archived sheet |
| **Checksum** | A digital fingerprint of a file, used to detect any later alteration |
| **Graduand record** | One graduand's final result: CGPA, class of degree, thesis and courses |
| **Indexing** | Typing the results from a scanned legacy sheet into the portal |
| **Legacy** | Old results digitized from the paper archive |
| **Locked** | Final; cannot be edited except by amendment |
| **Maker-checker** | The rule that the person who prepares something cannot also approve it |
| **QA (quality assurance)** | The independent check of an indexed sheet against its scan |
| **Result sheet** | A set of results for one programme and session, with its scan |
| **Row fingerprint** | A short code calculated from a sheet's results, printed on signed sheets to show they match the data |
| **Source scan** | The scanned signed and stamped paper sheet; the legal source of the results |
| **Two-step verification (MFA)** | Signing in with a password plus a code from an authenticator app |
| **Verification code** | The 8-character code on an issued document, checked on the public verification page |
